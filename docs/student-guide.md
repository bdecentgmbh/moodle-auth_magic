# Magic authentication — Student guide

On this site you can log in with a link that you get by email, instead of a password.

## Getting a link

1. On the login page, type your email address in the *Username* field. If the site allows it, your
   username works too.
2. Click *Magic link via email* (or *Log in with Magic link via email*).
3. Open the email and click the link. You are logged in and taken to the page you wanted.

If you click the button without typing anything, a page asks for your email address.

The page always answers "If you supplied a correct email address, an email containing a magic login
link should have been sent to you", whether the address belongs to an account or not. If no email
arrives, check your spam folder and the address you typed. You get at most one email every few
minutes, so wait a little before you ask again.

## Using the link

- The link works for a limited time, usually 10 minutes. The email tells you how long.
- On many sites a link logs you in only once. To log in again, ask for a new link.
- If you open a link that has expired, the site sends you a new one.
- If a link no longer works because it was used or replaced, the login page tells you so. Ask for a
  new link.
- Don't forward your link: whoever has it can log in as you while it works.

## Logged in as somebody else

If you open a link while somebody else is logged in on the same browser, you are asked whether to log
them out and log in as you. Click *Cancel* if you didn't expect this, for example because the link
came from somebody else.

## Accounts with a password

If your account uses a password, the site may email you a reminder to log in with your password
instead, with a link to reset it.
